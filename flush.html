How to play

Get from today's word to POOP. Change exactly one letter per move, and every step has to be a real four-letter word.

Example: TUNE → TONE → TOME → POME → POMP → POOP.

Par is the shortest possible route. Hints show which letter to change first; press again to see the word. A new daily puzzle arrives at midnight UTC, and Practice gives you unlimited random ones.
